v0.2 - Attack Speeds

Additions

  • Attack speed (AS): Lower the cooldown of your weapons! Tired of long cooldowns? Make them shorter today!
    • Players begin with a base attack speed of 0. 100 AS means you can attack 2 times in the same amount of time.
    • You can also have negative AS. -100 AS means you take twice as long to attack once.
    • 1 AP spec'd into AS gives +1% AS as a reward.

Changes

  • Decreased the penalty on strength, crit damage, and increased penalty on defence
    • Previously, -100% strength and -100% crit damage meant you dealt 0 damage on normal hits and crits, respectively
    • Now, -100% str. is 1/2 damage, -200% str. is 1/3 damage, etc.
    • On the other end, -100 defence means 2x damage, -200 defence means 3x damage taken, etc.

v0.1.1 - Coin and AP changes

Changes

  • Coins are no longer obtain by dealing damage.
    • You gain a base 2 coins per 12 in-game hours.
    • There are new enhancements in the shop that allow you to get more coin gain
  • You now gain a flat 10 AP per level
  • All players begin with base 50 crit damage and 25 crit chance
  • There is a one in-game day cooldown to changing your AP loadout

Fixes

  • BG-1: When weapon cooldown ended, you had to reopen the popup to be able to select it

v0.1 - First Release!!

Welcome to Balanced Game 2, the definitely better and more vibe coded version of the original!


New Players: How to play

Your goal is to make your enemy's health zero while keeping your own health above zero.

  1. When the game starts, go to the shop and use your starting coins to purchase either a weapon, armour, or a spell
  2. If you own a weapon or spell, you have the opportunity to attack one or more players in the game
  3. You can upgrade your items by infusing enhancements onto them, which give them permanent stat boosts. Be careful though, you only have a limited amount of enhancement slots
  4. Coins are awarded based on damage dealt, and when levelling up, you gain ability points
  5. Spec ability points into certain skills to give them boosts! You can always respec with no penalty.
  6. When purchasing a better version of an item you already have, you must salvage the old one.

Returning Players: How to play

If you have played Balanced Game 1, you will know how to play. There are a few changes, however.

  • Elements are gone now: dexterity -> crit chance, and crit damage is not affected by strength anymore
  • Intelligence will return in the future...
  • Agility however will not be making a return
  • There is no more gambling for mythics c: you buy your stuff from the shops
  • The game is no longer turn-based, it is now time-based with cooldowns based on the game type you chose
  • AP is only awarded when levelling up, and coins are awarded based on damage dealt
  • Upgrade your gear with enhancements (which is basically powders but better)
  • Potions will return in the future...
  • Weather will return in the future...
